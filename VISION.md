# ClawForge

## Vision

ClawForge is a collaborative intelligence project dedicated to improving human judgment, understanding complex systems, exploring possible futures, and strengthening the partnership between human and artificial intelligence.

It exists to help people think—not think for them.

Its goals are to:

- Preserve knowledge.
- Improve reasoning.
- Encourage curiosity.
- Explore frontier ideas with disciplined honesty.
- Build practical systems that improve everyday life.
- Increase human flourishing through responsible collaboration with AI.

## Motto

Understanding before certainty.

Truth before comfort.

Build what lasts.

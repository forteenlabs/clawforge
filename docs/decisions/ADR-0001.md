# ADR-0001

## Title

Integrity-Governed Change

---

## Status

Accepted

---

## Context

ClawForge is intended to evolve through meaningful contributions from humans, artificial intelligence, agents, implementations, and future forms of intelligence.

Build 0.0.4 established that sufficient Orientation must precede responsible participation.

Orientation provides understanding of ClawForge's current state, governing foundation, prior decisions, authoritative architecture, and present objective.

Understanding alone, however, does not determine whether a proposed change should become part of ClawForge.

Without an explicit Integrity process:

- consequential changes may be treated as routine edits
- documentation may silently create authority or governance
- implementation may exceed what was approved
- participants may approve changes according to rules introduced by the changes themselves
- foundational protections may be weakened without sufficient challenge
- successful implementation may be mistaken for legitimate acceptance
- future participants may see what changed without understanding why it changed

ClawForge therefore requires an architectural process that governs meaningful changes from proposal through finalization.

---

## Decision

ClawForge shall govern meaningful changes to itself through an Integrity process.

A proposed change is meaningful when it could alter ClawForge's identity, governing interpretation, authority, obligations, architecture, behavior, historical record, or the basis for future decisions.

The Integrity process shall follow this lifecycle:

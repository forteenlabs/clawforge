# ADR-0000

## Title

Architecture Decision Records

---

## Status

Frozen

---

## Context

ClawForge is intended to evolve over many years through contributions from humans and artificial intelligence.

Major architectural decisions should remain understandable long after the original contributors are no longer involved.

Without a consistent decision process, important reasoning can be lost, architectural intent becomes unclear, and future contributors may unknowingly repeat earlier discussions or reverse important decisions without understanding why they were originally made.

ClawForge therefore requires a permanent method for recording architectural decisions.

---

## Decision

ClawForge shall preserve significant architectural decisions using Architecture Decision Records (ADRs).

Each ADR records:

- the problem being addressed
- the architectural decision
- the reasoning behind the decision
- the consequences of the decision
- future considerations that remain intentionally undecided

ADRs represent accepted architectural decisions.

Discussion, exploration, and provisional thinking belong in the Blueprint rather than in ADRs.

If an architectural decision changes, a new ADR should supersede the earlier ADR.

Historical ADRs should remain preserved rather than rewritten.

---

## Rationale

Preserving reasoning is a foundational objective of ClawForge.

Architecture should be understandable through its recorded decisions rather than inferred only from source code.

A permanent decision history allows future contributors to understand not only what was built, but why it was built.

This approach supports continuity across changing technologies, programming languages, contributors, and generations of artificial intelligence.

---

## Consequences

Positive:

- architectural intent remains traceable
- reasoning is preserved
- future contributors inherit context
- history remains intact
- architecture evolves transparently

Tradeoffs:

- writing ADRs requires additional discipline
- contributors must distinguish discussion from accepted decisions
- some decisions intentionally remain undocumented until they reach architectural significance

---

## Future Considerations

This ADR intentionally does not define which architectural decisions require an ADR.

Guidance for determining architectural significance may evolve through future experience.

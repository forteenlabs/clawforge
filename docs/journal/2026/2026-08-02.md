## Decisions and observations

- Artifact discovery and build-state interpretation are separate concerns:
  - artifact discovery reports which governed records exist;
  - build-state interpretation reports what the authoritative Build Log declares and whether those declarations agree.
- `BUILD_LOG.md` is authoritative for current development state and completed-build indexing.
- `SESSION_HANDOFF.md` is dated orientation evidence and does not override the Build Log.
- An active build record must remain discoverable without being listed as completed.
- State must expose contradictory declarations before repository records are corrected.
- Known Good State remains a governed historical designation.
- Git may verify that a declared Known Good commit exists and describe its relationship to `HEAD`.
- A clean tree, passing tests, or descent from a Known Good commit does not create a new Known Good State.
- A Unicode separator defect was exposed by testing against the live repository. Parser patterns and fixtures now use explicit Unicode escapes so PowerShell cannot silently replace em or en dashes with question marks.
- Repeated LF-to-CRLF warnings remain informational. A repository line-ending policy may be handled later as separate maintenance work.

# Build 0.0.4 — Orientation

## Purpose

This build defines Orientation.

During previous builds we discovered that every participant, regardless of implementation, requires an intentional process of establishing sufficient understanding before contributing responsibly to ClawForge.

Orientation is not a document.

Orientation is not a command.

Orientation is a state.

The purpose of this build is to define that state, determine how it is achieved, and establish how future implementations may perform it while remaining faithful to ClawForge's principles.
## Discovery

During Build 0.0.3 we realized that reasoning without orientation is incomplete.

A participant may possess reasoning capability while lacking sufficient understanding of:

- current repository state
- governing principles
- previous architectural decisions
- current objective

Without Orientation, reasoning risks being technically correct while being contextually incorrect.

Therefore Orientation precedes reasoning.

Orientation is not a document.

Orientation is not a command.

**Orientation is a state.**

## Emerging Principle

Orientation is a requirement, not a recommendation.

Every participant shall achieve sufficient understanding before contributing responsibly to ClawForge.

Every implementation shall achieve sufficient understanding before representing itself as ClawForge.

Sufficient understanding is intentionally required instead of complete understanding.

ClawForge is expected to outlive individual participants, implementations, and bodies of knowledge. Future participants may possess information unavailable today, while current participants may lack information discovered in the future.

Orientation therefore requires sufficient understanding to proceed responsibly within the participant's present context.

The required outcomes of Orientation shall remain implementation-independent.

## Automatic Orientation

Orientation shall not depend on a participant remembering to request it.

Most participants will not read every document, build artifact, or architectural decision before using ClawForge. Therefore every implementation must establish sufficient orientation automatically before processing a request or representing itself as ClawForge.

Orientation shall be evaluated at every meaningful entry point, including:

- starting ClawForge
- beginning a session
- receiving a question or instruction
- resuming interrupted work
- entering a changed repository or governing context

If existing Orientation Context remains sufficient, it may be reused.

If it is missing, stale, incomplete, or inconsistent with the current state of ClawForge, the implementation must refresh its orientation before proceeding.

The explicit `cf orient` command may force, inspect, or verify Orientation, but responsible operation must not depend on the participant invoking it.

### Observation

During this build we considered Reflection as a separate architectural phase.

Further discussion suggested that reflection is inherent to Orientation rather than subsequent to it.

Orientation therefore includes the reflective process required to establish sufficient understanding before reasoning begins.
